About Me

I am a Data & AI solutions architect and applied AI engineer who turns complex business problems into scalable, governed, production-grade data and AI platforms. I design end-to-end architectures on Microsoft Fabric — ingestion, Lakehouse and Medallion layers, Spark, semantic models, and Power BI — and I embed AI directly into the data foundation, so that insights are automated, persistent, and trusted rather than locked inside a chat window.

I lead work from idea to roadmap to delivery: framing the problem with stakeholders, authoring target architectures and proofs of concept, setting naming standards and reusable templates, and acting as the technical escalation point when reporting, integration, or analytics issues get hard.

My foundation is research. I hold a PhD in Computer Science, awarded With High Honors, with work in bio-inspired AI, biometric authentication, and machine-learning optimization, published with IEEE, Elsevier, and Springer (160+ citations, h-index 7). That rigor shapes how I engineer: clear hypotheses, measurable validation, confidence scoring, and humans kept in the loop.

I am a frequent speaker at professional and research conferences, including EDUCAUSE, the Tennessee Higher Education IT Symposium (THEITS), the DATA xUTK Conference, and the UT Innovative Technologies Symposium, presenting on AI-driven data quality, embedded AI for decision intelligence, and agentic analytics.

01 AI Solution Initiative · Strategy & Architecture

AI-Powered Project Portfolio Intelligence

Business problem: Dashboards report the past, while early warning signs stay buried in status updates and risk logs. Vendor AI is locked inside individual tools, and chat copilots have to be prompted again before every meeting.

Solution: A production, end-to-end Microsoft Fabric architecture in which AI is part of the data model. Insights are generated on a schedule, stored as data, and served to leaders through a governed semantic model, a Power BI app, and agentic alerts.

Value: Earlier risk visibility, portfolio-wide consistency, and automated insight with no extra AI licensing.

02 AI Solution Initiative · Strategy & Architecture

AI-Driven Data Cleaning & Semantic Standardization

Business problem: Data cleaning can consume up to 80% of an analyst's time. Free-text notes and inconsistent categorical fields are the hardest part, and they affect reporting and governance.

Solution: A governed Medallion pipeline in Fabric. LLMs correct free text at scale, while RAG, embeddings, and cosine similarity map messy values to a curated reference library. Confidence scoring sends uncertain cases to people. It grew from my 2024 Python/Streamlit prototype into a scalable design.

Value: Less manual cleaning, faster data readiness, and more trust in analytics, with human expertise kept in the loop.

  • Medallion
  • Embeddings
  • Cosine Similarity
  • Human-in-the-loop
  • Python
  • Streamlit
03 AI Solution Initiative · Strategy & Architecture

Survey Feedback Intelligence

Business problem: Open-ended survey comments hold valuable insight, but manual analysis can take months, and chatbot answers change with every prompt.

Solution: A Fabric data app built on engineered prompts that run the same way on every comment and every term. AI classifies sentiment and themes, summarizes feedback by group and term, and a RAG-grounded executive advisor links findings to strategic goals.

Value: Reliable, comparable insight in place of months of manual review, with responsible AI built in: no invented numbers, privacy protected, every result verifiable.

  • Fabric Data App
  • Rayfin SDK
  • AI Functions
  • RAG
  • Responsible AI
04 AI Solution Initiative · Strategy & Architecture

AI Major Recommendation for Freshmen

Business problem: Choosing a major is one of the hardest early decisions for students, and traditional advising can feel impersonal.

Solution: An LLM-powered app that turns a student's interests, skills, and goals into personalized major, program, and career recommendations, with clear explanations. The roadmap adds a machine-learning model trained on survey data.
